# What's new in KidView

Add a section at the top for each release, headed `## <version>`. When you release, that
section becomes the release notes families see before they choose to update.

## 1.0.0

The first public version.

- A kids' app for Android that only shows what your rules allow: no ads, comments or "up next".
- Block videos, channels, words and whole topics (Roblox, scary games, brainrot memes, pranks).
- Filtered mode (anything not blocked) or Approved mode (only channels you allow).
- Schedules for when watching is on or off, with "allow for 30 minutes" when you need it.
- Different restrictions for each child's device.
- An offline library that saves videos onto the kids' devices for car trips and flights.
- Recommendations that start with funny and educational picks, then follow what each child likes.
- Activity for the past week (or longer, if you choose), with filters.
- The KidView Parent app for your phone, with parent accounts and a change log.
- Set up a device by scanning a QR code.
- The apps tell you when a new version is out, and you choose when to update.
