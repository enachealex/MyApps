How it works

A server in your home

KidView runs on a computer you already have: a Windows PC, or a home server, NAS or Raspberry Pi. Your rules, your kids' activity and saved videos stay on it.

The kids' app

Kids search and watch through your server, which hides anything your rules don't allow. No ads, no comments, no "up next", no links out.

You're in charge

Manage everything from the dashboard in any browser, or from your phone: the KidView Parent app on Android, or KidView added to your iPhone's Home Screen. Each parent has their own name and PIN.

Set it up

  1. Install the server

    Windows: unzip the download and double-click Install KidView. It installs what it needs, starts with Windows, and shows a purple icon by the clock. When Windows asks, allow KidView on private networks.
    Docker: run the command above.

  2. Create your parent account

    On the KidView PC, open http://localhost:8765/admin and add yourself under Home → Parent accounts. With Docker, open http://<server address>:8765/admin from your phone or PC and use the one-time setup code the installer showed.

  3. Set up each kid's device

    On the child's Android phone or tablet, point the Camera at the code on your dashboard under Home → Set up a kid's device. It opens a page that downloads KidView (Android asks once to allow installs from the browser, and Play Protect may ask you to confirm because it's not from the Play Store) and then opens it with your home's details filled in. Name the device and tap Connect.

  4. Put KidView on your phone

    iPhone: point the Camera at the small code under Home → Parent accounts on your dashboard. KidView opens in Safari; tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and sign in with your name and PIN. It opens full screen like an app.
    Android: install the parent app, tap Scan code, scan the same code, and sign in.

  5. Lock the devices down

    KidView only helps if it's the only way to YouTube. With Google Family Link (free), hide the YouTube app, YouTube Kids and web browsers, and require your approval for new apps.

Updates

  • KidView checks for new versions quietly and only asks when there is one. Tap Update or Later. Nothing updates without you.
  • The apps download and install the update themselves. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs from KidView, and Google Play Protect may ask you to confirm because KidView isn't from the Play Store.
  • For the server, press Update now on the dashboard or in the parent app's Settings. It restarts by itself in about a minute.
  • Want to check right away? Use Check for updates in the parent app's Settings, in the kids' app's grown-up setup (long-press the KidView title), or at the bottom of the dashboard.
  • Your settings, activity and saved videos are kept through every update.

Privacy

  • There's no KidView account and no cloud service. Everything KidView knows stays on the computer you install it on.
  • Your KidView server talks to YouTube to search and stream, and checks this website for new versions. The apps only talk to your own server, plus this website for app updates. No analytics, no ads.
  • KidView reaches YouTube with the open-source tool yt-dlp, which isn't an official YouTube service, so KidView is meant for personal use at home.
